## Policies
- Quotations: from English authors → restore the original English where it can be found, else translate; others
  translated fresh ({{STANDARD_TERMS}} terminology). Bible: {{BIBLE_POLICY}}.
- Latin / Greek / French in the text: {{FOREIGN_POLICY}} (e.g. kept as printed + [English gloss] on first use).
- Names: modernised in the English (scan stays as printed); Italianised/Latinised forms → standard forms.
- References: "§ 54", "p. 330", "vol. I"; abbreviations of the period expanded ({{ABBREVIATIONS}}).
- Divine pronouns: {{PRONOUNS}}. Capitalisation of nouns: English norms.
- Spelling: American. Misprints: an evident non-word → corrected silently; a real word or variant → kept, noted.
- Errata list (if printed): applied silently in the English; the errata page itself translated.
- Running heads translated as printed (never "corrected"); printer's signatures and sheet lines kept as printed.
